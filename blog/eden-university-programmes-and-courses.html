Eden University Programmes and Courses Offered

Admissions Information & Application Support Guide • Lusaka, Zambia

Home > Admissions Guides > Eden University Programmes and Courses Offered

Eden University is one of Zambia’s leading higher learning institutions, recognized for academic excellence, modern practical training, and regulatory accreditation. Discover all courses and programmes offered across our six academic schools.

1. School of Medicine & Health Sciences

Accredited by the Higher Education Authority (HEA) and Health Professions Council of Zambia (HPCZ):

  • Bachelor of Medicine & Bachelor of Surgery (MBChB) • 6 Years • Full-Time
  • BSc in Clinical Medicine • 4 Years • Full-Time / Distance
  • BSc in Biomedical Sciences • 4 Years • Full-Time
  • BSc in Public Health • 4 Years • Full-Time / Distance
  • BSc in Environmental Health • 4 Years • Full-Time / Distance
  • Diploma in Clinical Medicine • 3 Years • Full-Time
  • Diploma in Environmental Health • 3 Years • Full-Time
  • Diploma in Biomedical Sciences • 3 Years • Full-Time

2. School of Nursing & Midwifery

Accredited by the Nursing and Midwifery Council of Zambia (NMCZ):

  • Bachelor of Science in Nursing • 4 Years • Full-Time / Distance
  • Bachelor of Science in Midwifery • 4 Years • Full-Time
  • Diploma in Registered Nursing • 3 Years • Full-Time
  • Diploma in Registered Midwifery • 3 Years • Full-Time
  • Diploma in Public Health Nursing • 3 Years • Full-Time

3. School of Pharmacy

  • Bachelor of Science in Pharmacy • 4 Years • Full-Time
  • Diploma in Pharmacy • 3 Years • Full-Time

4. School of Law

  • Bachelor of Laws (LLB) • 4 Years • Full-Time / Distance

5. School of Business & Information Technology

  • BSc in Information Technology • 4 Years • Full-Time / Distance
  • BSc in Cyber Security & Network Defense • 4 Years • Full-Time
  • BSc in Accounting and Finance • 4 Years • Full-Time / Distance
  • BSc in Business Management • 4 Years • Full-Time / Distance
  • BSc in Banking and Finance • 4 Years • Full-Time / Distance
  • BSc in Human Resources Management • 4 Years • Full-Time / Distance
  • BSc in Marketing • 4 Years • Full-Time / Distance

6. School of Education, Humanities & Social Sciences

  • Bachelor of Arts in Development Studies • 4 Years
  • Bachelor of Arts in Social Work • 4 Years • Full-Time / Distance
  • Bachelor of Arts in Guidance & Counselling • 4 Years
  • Secondary & Primary Education Degrees • 4 Years • Full-Time / Distance
  • Diploma in Teaching Methodology • 1 Year • Distance
