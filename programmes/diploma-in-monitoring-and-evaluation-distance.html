SCHOOL OF Education, Humanities & Social Sciences

Diploma in Monitoring and Evaluation

Advance your career with our rigorous academic programme.

Programme Overview

The Diploma in Monitoring and Evaluation is meticulously designed to provide a comprehensive understanding in its respective field. Students develop critical thinking and practical skills required for a successful career.

Minimum Entry Requirements

  • Minimum of Three (3) to Four (4) Grade 12 / O-Level Credits
  • English Language is mandatory

Conditional Admission

Applicants who do not fully meet the standard entry requirements may still be considered for conditional admission after review by the admissions team. We encourage all prospective students to submit their applications for assessment.
