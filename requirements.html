Academic Entry Requirements

Review the mandatory 'O' level entry qualifications for each school below before submitting your application.

Faculty Requirement

School of Medicine and Health Sciences

5 'O' level credits or better (A-C) including; English, Mathematics, Science, Biology and any other subject.

Faculty Requirement

School of Natural Sciences

5 'O' level credits or better (A-C) including; English, Mathematics, Science, Biology and any other subject.

Faculty Requirement

School of Pharmacy

5 'O' level credits or better (A-C) including; English, Mathematics, Science, Biology and any other subject.

Faculty Requirement

School of Nursing and Midwifery

5 'O' level credits or better (A-C) including; English, Mathematics and any Science plus 2 other subjects.

Faculty Requirement

School of Education, Humanities and Social Sciences

5 'O' level credits or better (A-C) including; English and any other 4 subjects.

Programme Requirement

BSc/Diploma in ICT

5 'O' level credits or better (A-C) including; English, Mathematics and any other 3 subjects.

Faculty Requirement

School of Law and Business

Business — All

5 'O' level credits or better (A-C) including; English, Mathematics and any other 3 subjects.

Law

5 'O' level credits or better (A-C) including; English and any other 4 subjects.
