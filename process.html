Step-by-Step

How Your Application Works

Everything is conducted 100% online so you don't have to travel to Lusaka in person to get admitted.

01

Choose Your Programme

Explore our catalogue of over 50 accredited degrees and diplomas across Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy, Law, Business, and Education. Check specific entry criteria and semester tuition schedules.

02

Complete Online Application Form

Fill out the official admissions form on this portal with your applicant details, preferred study mode (Full-Time or Distance e-Learning), and primary contact WhatsApp number.

03

Submit Academic Documents

Upload clear scans or photos of your Grade 12 / O-Level result statement, National Registration Card (NRC) or International Passport, and previous college transcripts (for degree transfers).

04

Pay Application Processing Fee

Transfer the non-refundable processing fee (ZMW 150 local / ZMW 600 international) via Airtel Money, MTN Mobile Money, or Mukuru/WorldRemit to confirm your application submission.

05

Receive Official Admission Offer

Our Admissions Committee reviews qualifications within 30 minutes to 24 hours. If eligible, your official Eden University Acceptance Letter is delivered directly via WhatsApp and email.

06

Registration & Enrollment

Travel to campus or log into the distance learning student portal, complete your semester tuition fee registration, and begin orientation week for the upcoming intake.

Document Checklist

Please have the following ready before starting:

  • Scanned Grade 12 / O-Level Results
  • NRC or International Passport Copy
  • Passport-Size Photograph
  • Proof of Application Processing Fee
