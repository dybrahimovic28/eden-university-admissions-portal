Institutional Excellence

Why Students Choose Eden University

A modern higher education institution built on academic integrity, national accreditation, and career advancement.

Fully Accredited Qualifications

Every degree and diploma is recognized by the Higher Education Authority (HEA), the Health Professions Council of Zambia (HPCZ), and the General Nursing Council (GNC).

Rapid 30-Minute Application Review

Our online admissions system ensures speedy credential assessment. Receive your official admission offer letter directly to WhatsApp and email in under 24 hours.

Flexible Learning Modes

Choose between traditional on-campus classes at our modern Lusaka campus or flexible distance e-learning designed for working professionals and regional students.

Distinguished Faculty

Learn from seasoned professors, experienced clinical doctors, practicing legal practitioners, and business researchers passionate about your career success.

Transparent Fee Schedules

Clear, fixed semester tuition fees with zero hidden charges. Flexible payment installment options and multi-currency conversion for international students across Africa.

Global & Regional Reach

Welcoming students from Zambia, Zimbabwe, Malawi, Botswana, Namibia, DR Congo, and beyond with dedicated study permit processing and orientation services.

Admissions Journey

Your 6-Step Path to Eden University

A simple, transparent, and completely digital enrollment workflow from qualification assessment to campus registration.

01

Choose Your Programme

Explore our academic catalogue of over 50 accredited degrees and diplomas across Medicine, Nursing, Law, and Education.

02

Complete Online Application

Fill out the official online application form with your personal details and intended study mode (Full-Time or Distance).

03

Submit Academic Documents

Upload clear scans of your NRC or International Passport alongside your Grade 12 / O-Level result slip or previous college transcripts.

04

Pay Application Processing Fee

Pay the official application processing fee (ZMW 150 for Zambian applicants / ZMW 600 for international applicants) to initiate verification.

05

Receive Official Admission Offer

The Admissions Committee reviews your qualifications within 30 minutes to 24 hours. Your official acceptance letter is issued directly.

06

Registration & Commencement

Complete your semester tuition registration and begin your university classes on campus or through the online learning portal.
