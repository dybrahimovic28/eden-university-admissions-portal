Admissions & Enrolment Services

Comprehensive admissions support tailored to guide Zambian and international students through every stage of enrolling at Eden University.

HOW WE ASSIST YOU

Dedicated Student Support Services

From programme matching to document preparation and semester registration, we make your transition to Eden University effortless.

Online Application Assistance

We guide you step-by-step through completing your online application form. Our admissions officers in Lusaka verify your details to ensure completeness, preventing delays in issuing your acceptance.

  • Pre-submission error checking and document review
  • Direct coordination with faculty admissions committees
  • Real-time application status tracking via WhatsApp

Programme Selection Advisory

Choosing the right qualification is critical for your career. We analyze your academic strengths, O-Level/A-Level results, and professional aspirations to recommend the most suitable degree or diploma programme.

  • Subject combination evaluation for Medicine, Nursing & Pharmacy
  • Guidance on Full-Time vs. Distance Learning modes
  • Postgraduate and Diploma credit exemption advisory

Document Verification & Equivalency

Ensure your academic documents satisfy the Higher Education Authority (HEA) and professional council standards. We assist with both local ECZ statements and international credential conversions.

  • Clear document scanning guidelines
  • International qualification equivalency (Cambridge, ZIMSEC, Matric)
  • Conditional admission assessment for near-threshold candidates

Enrolment & Registration Guidance

Once your admission letter is issued, our advisory support continues through orientation, per-semester tuition schedules, accommodation options in Lusaka, and study permit processing for foreign nationals.

  • Semester tuition breakdown and payment instructions
  • Study permit and immigration clearance support
  • Campus orientation and course timetable preparation

Ready to Start Your University Journey?

Our Lusaka admissions team is ready to guide you. Submit your application online or connect directly on WhatsApp.
