Overview of the Eden University Application Process

Applying to Eden University is conducted fully online, allowing prospective students from Lusaka, all ten provinces of Zambia, and international applicants across Africa to submit their details smoothly without traveling in person.

Whether you are aiming to enroll in undergraduate degrees such as Medicine (MBChB), Nursing, Pharmacy, Law, ICT, or pursuing a Diploma or Postgraduate qualification, this portal assists you at every stage: from choosing your programme and verifying qualification requirements to completing your fee processing and securing your admission letter.

Who Can Apply?

Zambian School Leavers

Candidates possessing Grade 12 / O-Level certificates or statements of results from the Examinations Council of Zambia (ECZ) with 5 credits for degree programmes or 3-4 credits for diploma courses.

Diploma Holders & Mature Applicants

Working professionals or diploma graduates seeking degree progression, exemptions, or flexible distance-learning programmes while maintaining career commitments.

Postgraduate Applicants

Graduates holding recognized Bachelor’s degrees looking to advance with Master’s degrees or Postgraduate Diplomas in Public Health, Social Work, Education Management, or Teaching Methodology.

International Students

Students from Zimbabwe, Malawi, Botswana, Namibia, South Africa, and other nations holding Cambridge, ZIMSEC, Matric, BGCSE, or equivalent senior secondary credentials.

Documents Required for Your Application

To ensure timely processing of your application, have clear digital scans or photos of the following documents ready before submitting:

  • Certified Copy of Academic Results: ECZ Statement of Results, Grade 12 Certificate, Cambridge, ZIMSEC, or equivalent examination certificate.
  • National Identification: Copy of your National Registration Card (NRC) for Zambians, or valid Passport Bio-Data page for International applicants.
  • Passport-Size Photograph: Recent passport photo for student identification.
  • Previous Qualifications (if applicable): Official transcripts and certificates if applying for degree top-ups, diploma exemptions, or postgraduate study.
  • Proof of Application Processing Fee Payment: Mobile money transaction confirmation or transfer receipt.

Step-by-Step: How to Apply to Eden University

1

Select Your Desired Programme

Review the Eden University Programmes Database covering Medicine, Pharmacy, Nursing, Law, Business, Education, Natural Sciences, and Humanities. Choose your preferred study mode: Full-Time on campus in Lusaka or Distance Learning.

2

Verify Admission Requirements

Check the entry requirements on our Admission Requirements page. If your grades do not fully match, conditional admission review may be requested.

3

Complete the Online Application Form

Fill out the secure online application form on our Apply Online page with your personal details, selected programme, and contact phone/WhatsApp number.

4

Submit Required Documents & Fee

Provide proof of the application processing fee and submit your academic results and NRC/Passport copy for committee verification.

5

Receive Your Admission Decision

Once verified, successful applicants receive their official admission acceptance letter via WhatsApp and email, detailing registration dates and semester schedules.

Eden University Intakes: January & July

Eden University enrols students twice each academic year through two primary intakes:

  • January Intake: The main intake of the academic year for all schools, including competitive clinical programmes in Medicine (MBChB), Nursing, and Pharmacy.
  • July Intake: Mid-year admissions intake for undergraduate degrees, diplomas, and distance-learning programmes.

For full intake schedules and application guidelines, visit our dedicated January & July Intakes page.
